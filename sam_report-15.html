How the leverage scale works

Old reports score you 1 to 5 on risk. SAM scores you 1 to 5 on leverage — how strongly this system responds to what you do. That is not a softer version of the same number. It is a different number, because a system can be genetically average and still be the place where your habits move the needle furthest.

04 — Philosophy

Pleasure is not the opposite of health.

Most of what shows up in your bloods, your sleep and your recovery is built out of ordinary days. A meal made properly. A long conversation. Movement that feels good rather than movement you survive. These are not the soft part of health — they are the mechanism, and the science behind why is the reason this report exists.

What this report is based on

Half philosophy, half science — treated as one method

The field this report draws on is nutritional genomics and epigenetics: how dietary patterns and daily behaviour change the way genes are read, and how that shows up in ageing, recovery and disease risk over a life.

It is a real research literature, not a wellness idea. Randomised diet trials with epigenetic endpoints, systematic reviews of DNA methylation change across weight-loss interventions, and two decades of work on which nutrients and behaviours actually move those marks. SAM inherits the standard of evidence along with the ideas — which is why every marker in here carries a grade, including the weak ones.

The two halves this report is built on, and refuses to separate:

Philosophy

How simple pleasures — well-made healthy meals, conversations with friends, movement that feels good — enrich our lives.

Science

The provable lifestyle factors that can actually outsmart our genes and shape our lives. Epigenetics.

A report that only had the second half would be a lab printout. One that only had the first would be a poster.

Hardware you were given. Software you write.

Epigenetics is the software running on your DNA hardware — a layer of molecules sitting on top of your genes and turning them up or down like a dimmer rather than a switch. Chromatin has to open before a gene can be read at all, and what you eat supplies the chemistry that opens or closes it.

That is the same distinction SAM draws between your marker results and your daily data, and it is the spine of this whole report: the markers are hardware, everything else in SAM is the software layer.

Layer one — nutrigenetics

Your genes change the questions worth asking.

Inherited differences can affect how nutrients are absorbed, transported and used. A PEMT or vitamin-D carrier result supplies context for intake and measured values; it does not establish a current need or dose. This is the layer the marker tabs read.

Layer two — epigenetics

What you eat changes how genes are read.

Nutrients supply the chemistry for methylation, and food compounds regulate the enzymes that write and erase epigenetic marks. This is the layer the dial on the next tab describes — and the one you control.

The food compounds that talk to your genes fall into two families: methyl donors, which supply the raw chemistry for methylation, and epi-bioactives, which regulate the enzymes that write and erase epigenetic marks. The three simple pleasures

Named in the philosophy line above, and not the soft part of health. They are the mechanism.

Well-made healthy meals

A meal, not a protocol.

The compounds that reach your genes arrive through ordinary cooking — greens, eggs, olive oil, brassicas, fish, herbs. From the kitchen, not the pharmacy. Every result in this report points at a plate before it points at anything else.

Conversations with friends

Longevity is social before it is biological.

The people who age well are rarely the ones tracking the most. They prioritise their people, move naturally, and find a daily reason to get dressed. Connection moves cortisol, sleep depth and inflammatory tone measurably. Your stress-axis markers describe how loudly your body answers; who you spend Sunday with changes what that answer costs.

Movement that feels good

If it does not feel good, it will not last.

Pleasure as a compass, applied to training: the best programme is the one still running in March. Your power and endurance markers exist to help you pick a kind of effort you will look forward to — not to hand you a prescription you will abandon.

05 — The science

Your genes are read out loud every day.

The sequence you were born with does not change. What changes — constantly, measurably, within weeks — is how loudly each gene is read. Methyl groups land on DNA and quieten a gene. Histone tags loosen or tighten the spool it is wound on. That layer is called the epigenome, and it takes instructions from sleep, food, movement, light, alcohol, and stress.

Where the evidence for each marker comes from

Every marker in SAM carries a grade, and the grade is per claim — not per gene. The same gene can be graded B for what the enzyme does and D for whether one SNP predicts your performance. Collapsing those into one number is how genetic reports overclaim.

A replicated in randomised or large prospective human studies B consistent human functional or phenotype evidence, replicated C mixed or single-population human evidence, biologically coherent D mechanistic or candidate-gene only — direction not established grade = how sure we are (never moves a score on its own) weight = how much it may move (separate lever, capped by design)

SAM reports the grade next to every result, including the weak ones, rather than quietly dropping them. A D-grade marker that shapes nothing still tells you something about how the field works.

08 — Sleep

Sleep is the centre of this report, not a chapter of it.

Every other system in SAM is read against your sleep. Your clock genes set when your body wants to be awake, your adenosine and caffeine genes set how sleep pressure builds and clears, and your arousal genes set how easily you come back down at night. Get the timing right and half the other systems relax on their own.

Histamine, sleep and how you feel

Histamine across one night — yours.

The curve is histamine load from dinner through to morning, shaped by how fast your own two gates clear it. Where your results predict something, it is marked on the curve at the hour it happens. The methyl-budget box is the link back to the methylation figure: HNMT is a methyltransferase, so clearing histamine at night is paid for out of the same budget.

What this loop does and does not say. The well-evidenced link is sleep to mood: short and fragmented nights measurably affect next-day mood, focus and emotional control, and the effect compounds across a week. Histamine is one of several things that can fragment a night, and your clearance genotypes describe a tendency rather than an outcome. None of this diagnoses anything, and no genotype here causes a mental health condition. If your mood is troubling you, that is worth taking to a person rather than to a pathway diagram — and it is worth doing whatever this figure says.
Marker detail
Why sleep is the anchor and not just another domain

SAM's engine compares you to yourself, not to a population average — an N=1 design. Sleep is the anchor for that comparison because it is the one stream that (a) recurs every 24 hours, (b) precedes the outcome rather than following it, and (c) has a measured, device-verifiable form. A change in your recovery, mood or readiness is read against the nights that came before it.

anchor = last night's sleep (stage mix, duration, continuity, onset latency) window = rolling 7 / 28 nights, your own baseline only rule = no domain flag fires without an anchored sleep window missing = if the anchor is absent, the domain reports "not evaluated" — never "clear"

Two streams SAM needs from a band and does not get from every model: sleep onset latency and respiratory rate. Where they are missing, the affected rules report as unevaluated rather than quietly passing.

Marker detail

Food first, always

Your called markers can put a nutrient on a review shortlist. They cannot prove that you need it or calculate your dose. Food intake, a recognised measurement and safety context decide what happens next.

What this means in practice. A methylation result of any kind does not earn you a B-vitamin protocol. It earns you a specific question to ask at your next blood draw. If that draw shows a real shortfall, and food cannot close it, and low intake is genuinely the cause, then there is a product conversation — with a retest date and a stopping rule attached to it. Iron and B12 stay clinician-gated regardless.

13 — What's necessary

One conclusion, drawn from the whole report.

No marker in SAM recommends anything by itself. A single result is an input, and an input is not a reason to take something. This page runs after every system has been read, looks for the places where several results across different systems point at the same thing, and returns the short list that is actually necessary for you.

Short is the point. If the list were long, it would be wrong.

Supplements live on their own tab. They are a different kind of decision from everything above — one that needs a gate, a dose, a review date and, sometimes, a clinician's signature. Keeping them here would have made them look like the natural end of a genetic report, and they are not.

How the synthesis decides

The unit of recommendation is the person, not the marker. Nothing reaches the list from one gene, however strong that gene's evidence grade is.

for each candidate need: score = Σ (marker leverage − 1) × weight over CALLED markers only domains = distinct SAM systems those markers sit in behaviour / food appears if score ≥ 3 AND ≥ 3 distinct markers measurement appears if score ≥ 2 AND ≥ 2 distinct markers safety item appears on one marker, by exception, and is always routed to a clinician supplement appears only after explicit intake identifies a food-first gap, or recognised pathology confirms a shortfall, after contraindication checks. genetics supplies context, never need or dose. iron and B12 remain clinician-gated. caps: 3 behaviours + 2 foods, 5 measurements. Ranked, then cut. everything cut is listed with its score, so you can argue with it.

Iron and vitamin B12 stay clinician-gated even after a number crosses a trigger, because both have a wrong-direction failure mode that a genotype cannot see. Everything else that unlocks still names food or sunlight first, and gives the shortfall a defined period to close before anything is bought.

20 — SAM agent

Ask it anything. It cannot make anything up.

SAM's agent is built in two layers on purpose. Underneath is a deterministic retriever that looks your answer up in versioned, auditable rules — the same objects that produced your report. On top, optionally, sits a language model whose only job is to phrase what the retriever found. It is not permitted to author a rule, invent a threshold, or answer from its own knowledge.

Answers are retrieved from your loaded profile. With no profile, the agent answers about the rules themselves rather than about you — and says so.

The agents behind the report

Sixteen agents under one supervisor. Eight call a model. Eight are ordinary deterministic code and never touch one. Permissions are separated — an agent that can read your genotypes cannot message your coach.

The rule that governs all of them

No clinical rule may exist only inside a prompt. Every threshold, every band, every reason a flag fires lives in a version-controlled knowledge object with a valid-from date. The model receives that object and phrases it. If the model is unreachable, the retriever answers alone and the report is unchanged — which is why SAM works fully offline.

member question → retriever finds knowledge object(s) by domain + marker + intent → deterministic answer assembled from object fields → [optional] model rewrites for register (member / coach / clinician) → language gate strips blocked words → safety layer: any red-flag intent routes to a person, not a phrase

Why some numbers your band shows never reach this report

Wearables ship their own composite scores — a recovery score, a stress index, a biological age. Those are computed by a formula SAM cannot inspect, cannot version, and cannot explain to a clinician. They are quarantined at the boundary: stored, visible to you in the band's own app, and excluded from every SAM score.

ACCEPTED raw + derived physiological signals HR · HRV (RMSSD) · SpO₂ · skin temp · sleep stages steps · activity minutes · respiratory rate · onset latency QUARANTINED vendor composites recovery.score · stress.dailyIndex · bioAge glucose risk index · PTT-derived blood-pressure trend REASON an unauditable number cannot carry a clinical decision

SAM also reports what your specific device can and cannot measure. Where a sensor is absent, the rules that depend on it say not evaluated — never clear. A missing measurement is not a normal one.
